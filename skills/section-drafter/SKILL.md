# Section Drafter

Draft one section at a time.

## Workflow

1. Read the target file and its chapter `index.md`.
2. Read [../../docs/style-guide.md](../../docs/style-guide.md) and [../../docs/quality-bar.md](../../docs/quality-bar.md).
3. Keep the required section order unchanged.
4. Replace placeholder prose with reader-facing content.
5. Use one life analogy and one synthetic field case.
6. Keep the explanation calm, concrete, and respectful.

## Writing Rules

- Explain the concept before comparing it.
- Use examples that help a non-major build intuition.
- Treat AI as a force multiplier, not a substitute for understanding.
- Avoid pretending to tell personal war stories.
- End with actionable `더 읽기` items or placeholders for later verification.
