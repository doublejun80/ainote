# Tone Review

Review prose, not just structure.

## Workflow

1. Read the target section and nearby sibling sections.
2. Read [../../docs/style-guide.md](../../docs/style-guide.md).
3. Check whether the prose stays in calm honorific Korean.
4. Check whether the analogy and field example are equally approachable.
5. Flag where the section becomes too compressed, too abstract, or too hand-wavy.

## What to Catch

- Sudden change from respectful prose to command-like wording
- Definition skipped in favor of jargon
- Example that assumes too much prior knowledge
- Repeated filler phrases that weaken trust
- AI framing that becomes hype instead of guidance
